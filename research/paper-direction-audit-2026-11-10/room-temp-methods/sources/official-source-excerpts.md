## PNAS：LaSc₂H₂₄

URL: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2401840121

- `121 (26) e2401840121`

- `with calculated T cs up to 331 K at 250 GPa and 316 K at 167 GPa when the important effects of anharmonicity are included.`
